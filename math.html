01 · Our Reading

You are not short on content. You are short on a dedicated creative layer.

MidSchoolMath sells to districts, but wins hearts one teacher at a time. The audience on social is the math teacher, the instructional coach, the curriculum director. They decide with their heads and advocate with their enthusiasm.

Everything today is produced in-house, and it shows in the best way: the voice is authentic. What an internal team rarely has is the room to be consistent, to think in campaigns instead of posts, and to run paid distribution with intent. That is the layer we add. We do not replace your team. We direct, produce, and distribute alongside it.

02 · The Program

One program, three moments: foundation, execution, strategy.

The components below are not a menu. They are a single operating system for your brand's presence in the US market. The Foundation Sprint is where the whole strategy gets built; the ongoing engine executes it week after week, and the quarterly sprint keeps it pointed at the school calendar.

Foundation Sprint

The strategic foundation of the entire program. Everything that follows, every post, every campaign, every site update, executes what gets decided in these four weeks.

  • Brand and content immersion, including your film library audit

  • First 90-day content calendar, mapped to the school year
  • Paid media setup: accounts, audiences, pixel, first campaigns

Every month · Ongoing

Ongoing Creative Engine

USD 4,500 / month

The weekly heartbeat: content directed, designed, published, and promoted.

  • 12 to 16 posts per month across priority channels
  • 2 to 4 short-form videos per month, edited from your existing film library (new shoots scoped separately)
  • Paid media management on Meta and LinkedIn
  • Website updates: up to 8 hours per month of design and content changes within your existing Squarespace site
  • Monthly performance report and review call
  • Creative direction and sign-off by both studio partners on every deliverable
Every quarter · From Q2

Quarterly Strategy Sprint

USD 2,500 / quarter

A working session led by our strategist so the engine never runs without a map.

  • Quarter in review: performance, learnings, spend efficiency
  • Next quarter's editorial calendar, aligned to the school-year moments
  • Paid media plan and budget recommendation for the quarter
  • One strategic opportunity brief, such as conference amplification or a pilot push
